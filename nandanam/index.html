— About Kreate by Kraft • Nandanam

More Than A Café.

Situated on Anna Salai, Kreate by Kraft Nandanam bridges the gap between productive co-working and exquisite culinary hospitality. Featuring high-speed connectivity, live-edge communal tables, and specialty coffee laboratories.

Every corner is curated to spark inspiration, whether you are hosting a client breakfast, drafting your next enterprise, or enjoying a slow afternoon with kindred spirits.
