— About The Brunch Theory

More Than A Brunch.

Nestled on Chennai's most iconic boulevard, Khader Nawaz Khan Road, The Brunch Theory is an intentional retreat from the hurried pace outside. Designed with earthy terracotta tones, hand-finished lime-wash walls, and plush banquettes, our sanctuary celebrates the unhurried joy of sharing elevated food.

From sunrise flat whites made with micro-lot beans to golden brioche towers and intimate multi-course evening dinners, every plate honours culinary precision, vibrant seasonal produce, and soulful hospitality.
